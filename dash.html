Lovely Lane Dash

Two best friends. One lovely adventure.
Gather hearts and take turns leading the way.

How to Play

A/D or ←/→: lanes · W/↑/Space: jump · S/↓: slide · Q/Tab: swap · P/Esc: pause · M: mute.

🐼 Bubu: press jump again for a double jump; hold to glide briefly. Fits through pink narrow fences. Dodge crates and cracked rocks.

🐻 Dudu: one jump. Down + jump in the air ground-pounds; down fast-falls. Slide on the ground smashes crates and cracked rocks for bonus hearts.

Jump puddles and low fences; duck branches and arches; dodge thorns. Spring mushrooms launch you across short blue gaps. A hit costs one life, never a fight!

Hearts +10, honey +25. Consecutive hearts build up to ×5; missed hearts or hits reset the chain. Golden hearts give 5 seconds of Hug Mode: wider magnet, double points.

Touch: swipe to move, jump or slide. Use Swap to change lead; hold Jump to glide. Taps do not count as swipes. The glowing heart trail suggests a safe route.
